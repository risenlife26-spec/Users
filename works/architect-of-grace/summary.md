# Rolling Summary — *The Architect of Grace*

*Updated as new chapters arrive. Captures cross-chapter observations
that aren't visible from any single chapter.*

---

## As of architecture lock (2026-04-22)

### Voice

McCarthy lineage, Psalm 51 posture (not Psalm 23 / not Cormac
imitation). Plain register, located in the contemporary Central Valley.
Long polysyndetic sentences for landscape and interiority; short
declarative beats for character. The register is consistent and earned.

Guardrail per Editor Pass: polysyndeton must earn its keep — not a
default setting that masks missing sensory detail.

### Four Movements (canonical labels)

- I. The Soil
- II. The Gavel
- III. The Rebuilding
- IV. The Framing

See `architecture.md` for the chapter/interlude inventory.

### Core Engine

Cody turns back with open hands on White Lane and is murdered by Jaime,
who cannot tolerate unearned grace. Every scene prepares for, witnesses,
or is broken by this.

### Recurring motifs (landed across the manuscript)

- **Open hands** — Cody on the sidewalk (Ch 7); Lisa on the tile (Ch 15);
  Martin Kessler's Bible on his chest (Ch 6 recollection); Jaime on the
  bathroom floor (Ch 13); Blake in the grow room (Ch 5) and at the
  chapel (Ch 17). The posture of putting something down.
- **The floor holds** — Ch 5 ("he was not holding himself up; the floor
  was holding him"); Ch 17 ("The floor held."). *Priority scrub target:
  reduce total instances to 4 (see `status.md` item 4).*
- **Weight vs. release** — the gun vs. the Bible ("paper is lighter than
  steel; but it was heavier"); the gavel Rick put down; the weight
  Blake stopped needing (Ch 17 "I don't need the weight").
- **The gavel** — Rick's symbol; the law of loyalty; the thing Rick held
  instead of the thing he was supposed to hold.
- **Fourteen feet** — Blake's buffer habit; the "stone-center" rule.
  (Earlier exec report flagged ~37 occurrences; scrub toward essentials.)
- **Water / mirage** — the asphalt shimmer; ditches dry in September;
  Valley heat as a lie about water.
- **Hands** — Brittni's stitching hands (Ch 4); Rick's hands still on the
  kid's shoulder (Ch 10); Jaime's hand at ten and two; Blake's hands
  uncurling one finger at a time.

### Canonical Cast

- **Blake** (POV) — landscaping / grow-room maintenance; "Kayden" in
  Mickey's deep-history address.
- **Sarah** — Blake's wife; curator/organizer; builds EternityOS in
  Ch 14.
- **Brittni Morrison** — White Lane veterinarian; Blake's half-sister
  via David Morrison. **Canonical name: Brittni, not Lisa.** Manuscript-
  wide scrub required (see `status.md`).
- **Rick** — former MC president; runs Teen Challenge compound east of
  Bakersfield; killed seven men (see Interlude: Seven Shovels of Dirt).
- **Cody Reyes** — Blake's cousin; MC prospect; dies on White Lane
  (Ch 7) with open hands, facing north.
- **Jaime** — landscaper with a white F-150 and tres leches on the back
  seat; the man who shoots Cody (Ch 13 Peter confession).
- **Mickey Doyle** — the Seam; Blake's uncle. Surname Doyle. Father
  Patrick Doyle. *Not "the weather" — that framing appears in
  quarantined Altar of the Hallway and should be treated as voice
  violation.*
- **Patrick Doyle** — Mickey's father; load-rating line; endurance motif.
- **Elena** — Jaime's wife; the repairer (glues the broken bowl); hosts
  the dinner (Ch 11).
- **Diane** — Blake's mother; Brundage Lane kitchen; reads Revelation
  aloud.
- **Marilyn** — Patrick Doyle's 40-year accounting.
- **Titus** — Blake's dog; 130 pounds of black muscle and devotion.
- **Penny** — bait dog Cody brought to the clinic (referenced Ch 11);
  chained to a cinder block in her prior life.
- **Danny Salcido** — first man Rick killed (Delano garage, 2004).
- **Martin Kessler** — sixth man Rick killed; Bible open on his chest,
  Romans 5 underlined.
- **Margaret** — Lisa's/Brittni's mother in Fresno (Ch 16 postcard
  handoff).
