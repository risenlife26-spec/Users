## As of Chapter 1 partial (2026-04-20)

### Voice
McCarthy lineage, plain register, located in the contemporary Central
Valley. Long polysyndetic sentences for landscape and interiority;
short declarative beats for character ("Blake fixed. Cody replaced.
Blake maintained. Cody burned."). The register is consistent and
earned.

### Recurring motifs already visible
- **Water as mirage / absence** — the asphalt shimmer that lies about
  water; the water trough that doesn't matter to Titus.
- **Devotion-toward-absence** — Diane organizing her life around an
  absent father; Titus aimed at the gate where Blake usually appears.
- **Fixing vs. replacing vs. burning** — Blake / Cody / Mickey as
  three responses to the same world.
- **Watching from the edges** — Blake fourteen feet from the men
  speaking in codes.
- **Heat / pressure as company** — the only thing on the shoulder of
  the 5 with him.
- **Mechanical world** — Silverado, blown radiator, Dodge Ram with
  cracked windshield, eight-ball shift knob, AC broken since
  purchase. The book's metaphors live in this register.

### Cast established
- Blake (POV)
- Diane (Blake's mother)
- Mickey (Diane's brother — Vietnam vet, Teamster, "weather")
- Cody (Mickey's de facto son; Blake's cousin by proximity)
- Patricia (Cody's mother — Delano packing-shed worker)
- Titus (Blake's dog)
- Blake's father (absent, Reno)
