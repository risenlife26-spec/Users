## Mickey Doyle — The Inverted Trinity (canon)

- **Father (Autonomy)** — Mickey as the self-authorizing shaper; the
  man who refuses to be acted upon.
- **Spirit (Hands)** — the fused hand; fiberglass-as-virus; drywall as
  the medium of unseen damage.
- **Lamb (Silenced Conscience)** — the child in Mickey that was
  sacrificed to build the father; recoverable only by grace that
  Mickey himself cannot authorize.

The Altar of the Hallway quarantine entry collapses these three
pillars into a single "weather" shorthand and a monologue register
inconsistent with Mickey's canonical one-stone-center-line mode —
primary reason the file is quarantined, not canonized.
