# Quarantine

This folder preserves files that are NOT canon but should not be lost.

## What goes here

- AI-fabricated prose that carries Gemini/Copilot fingerprints
  (vocabulary drift, voice drift, canon-incompatible plot elements).
- Variant drafts of chapters that Travis has retired in favor of other
  openings or structures.
- Material whose provenance is uncertain pending verification.
- AI-assisted editorial material that offers useful diagnostic
  observations but is not the author's own editorial voice.

## What does NOT go here

- Early-draft Travis prose. Those live in `drafts/` if needed, or are
  simply superseded by canonical files in `manuscript/`.
- Reference material (legal, critical, theological). Those live in
  `reference/` or `feedback/`.
- Ghost Audit edit logs from the scrub pass (`status.md` item 1). Those
  are deleted, not preserved.

## Contents

- `the-altar-of-the-hallway.md` — chapter-length piece carrying
  multiple canon violations (Mickey-as-weather vs. canonical
  Mickey-as-Seam; multi-paragraph Mickey monologue vs. one-stone-center
  rule; "Lisa" name drift; Ch 10 closing-image verbatim lift; Emerson
  template narrator-applause; gavel-handle echo). Preserved archivally;
  not canonized. Provenance pending.

- `recovered-prose-movements-I-III-ch3.md` — long AI-fabricated
  Movements I–III + Ch 3 text with attached Gemini editorial
  commentary. Non-canonical sci-fi plot elements (bunker, twelve-
  million-dead Erasure, Sofia/stuffed-rabbit, Sarah-as-Blake's-wife in
  a dystopian register). Preserved so the fingerprint signature stays
  visible for future triage.

- `gemini-curator-and-architect-snippet.md` — bracketed [CHAPTER 1:
  THE CURATOR AND THE ARCHITECT] / [CHAPTER 2: THE PROSPECT]
  fragment Travis retired in favor of the Grapevine opening. Preserved
  to document the decision point.

## Why preserve at all

Quarantine is a discipline, not a trash bin. Keeping the contaminated
material visible:

1. Makes it harder for the same drift to re-enter the manuscript
   unnoticed.
2. Documents the decision record for future editorial passes.
3. Gives the canon-variants analysis (`feedback/canon-variants.md`) a
   concrete set of artifacts to point at.
