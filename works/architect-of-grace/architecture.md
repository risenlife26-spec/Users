# The Architect of Grace — Architecture

*Locked: 2026-04-22*

## Core Engine

Cody turns back with open hands on White Lane and is murdered by Jaime,
who cannot tolerate unearned grace. Every scene prepares for, witnesses,
or is broken by this.

## Four Movements

- **Movement I — The Soil**
- **Movement II — The Gavel**
- **Movement III — The Rebuilding**
- **Movement IV — The Framing**

The four-movement structure is canonical per the front-matter and the
Executive Editorial Report. An earlier paste proposed alternate labels
(Soil / Interior Collapse / External Confrontation / Inheritance) — those
were a working/diagnostic view and are not canonical.

## Canonical Cast

- **Blake** (POV) — landscaping / grow-room maintenance; formerly "Kayden"
  in Mickey's address.
- **Sarah** — Blake's wife; curator/organizer; EternityOS.
- **Brittni Morrison** — White Lane veterinarian; Blake's half-sister via
  David Morrison. Canonical name is **Brittni**, not Lisa (the Lisa name
  appears in deprecated drafts and AI-fabricated material — see
  `quarantine/`). Standard scrub required manuscript-wide.
- **Rick** — former MC president; now runs the Teen Challenge compound
  east of Bakersfield; killed seven men.
- **Cody Reyes** — Blake's cousin; prospect for the MC; dies on White
  Lane with open hands.
- **Jaime** — landscaper; man who kills Cody; unearned-grace figure.
- **Mickey Doyle** — the Seam (drywall / fiberglass-as-virus /
  fused-hand); Blake's uncle. Surname: Doyle. Father: Patrick Doyle.
- **Patrick Doyle** — Mickey's father; load-rating line; endurance.
- **Elena** — Jaime's wife; repairs broken things; hosts the dinner.
- **Diane** — Blake's mother; "the climate" to Mickey's weather (when
  that framing is used; see editorial notes).
- **Marilyn** — Patrick Doyle's 40-year accounting.
- **Titus** — Blake's dog; 130-pound black muscle and devotion.
- **Penny** — the bait dog Cody brought to the clinic; chained to a
