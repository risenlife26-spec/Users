# CHAPTER 13 // PETER

## MANUSCRIPT

Jaime knelt on the bathroom tile. Bare feet. White T-shirt soaked with sweat. His back rested against the bathtub, his hands gripping the linoleum.

"I killed Cody," Jaime whispered.

The words fell into the kitchen. The dog shifted under the table. The refrigerator hummed.

"He was turning back," Blake said.

"Yes."

Blake's fists tightened. The .38 rested on the table between them — two pounds of steel pulling the room's gravity into its cylinder. Blake stared at Jaime's bare feet on the tile. He looked at the gun. He looked at the closed, uncracked Bible on the counter. He thought of the grow room, the water soaking his knees.

His hands opened.

One finger at a time, the knuckles losing their bloodless white strain. His palms flattened against the wood grain. He picked up the .38. Jaime did not blink. Blake popped the cylinder and tipped the barrel. Six brass rounds clattered onto the table, rolling and clicking against each other in the low light. Blake set the empty gun down.

Jaime's shoulders collapsed. He wept, his mouth open, his body shuddering with ragged, tearing sounds. Blake sat across the table, his hands open on the wood, and watched the man break.
