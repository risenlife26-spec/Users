# 5 minutes

*Source: handwritten journal, page 2*
*Transcribed: 2026-04-20*
*Status: verbatim; opening fragment + main poem; continues onto next page*
*Author: Eligio Hernandez (signed)*

---

**[Opening fragment / epigraph]**

5 minutes

we spent all but
5 minutes.
my eyes are closed
was it worth it.
Yes.
5 min(utes!

By Eligio Hernandez

---

**[Main poem]**

5 minutes till this, and five more till that
life is lived in that five minutes
five present five past.
Five minute intervals with each crisis [unclear: crisis]
in step
all away from the shadows
all in darkness we've wept.
going to this spot, from that spot
to next,
Five minutes of joy turns to
