To my Jesus

The truth is that tonight I find myself in a
place that God alone could orchestrate. The flow-
ing river of events that constitute the whole of
my life, starting at the source leading on to
now. I realize there is no one like me and
equally as wonderful there is no one like you.
Each one of us each our own and one another.
The same. I think that I am less
prepared than I thought I was.
I jumped like always into a project
I couldn't finish, I always have
good plans that end with someone
broke and our existence diminished
