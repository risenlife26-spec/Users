# The Architect of Grace — Novel Architecture

**The Core Engine:** Cody turns back with open hands on White Lane, and is murdered by a man (Jaime) who cannot tolerate unearned grace. Every scene prepares for this, witnesses this, or is broken by this.

## Movement I: The Soil (The False Foundations)

- Chapter 1: The Curator and the Architect (Blake & Sarah established; the introduction of Jaime's unearned grace).
- Chapter 2: The Prospect (Rick's compound; Cody waiting to bleed).
- Chapter 3: White Lane (Cody meets Brittni).
- Chapter 4: The Mud (The Vagos hit; Mickey wraps a body).

## Movement II: The Interior Collapse

- Chapter 5: The Wrong Label (Blake misreads Jaime's pressure at Ewing Irrigation).
- Chapter 6: The Rose Bed (Jaime's shears lock in the living wood; Blake's compartments strain).
- Chapter 7: Salty's & The Dark Gap (Cody leaves the table, turns back, and is killed).

## Movement III: The External Confrontation

- Chapter 8: False Mercy (Jaime comforts Blake with poisoned, over-precise geometry).
- Chapter 9: Judgment (Blake investigates, the filing system fails, Sarah's clinic aftermath).
- Chapter 10: The Gavel (Blake confronts Rick with the gun; Rick refuses jurisdiction).

## Movement IV: The Inheritance

- Chapter 11: Peter (Jaime confesses; Blake empties the bullets).
- Chapter 12: The Walk (Blake walks four miles, retrieves the bait dog).
- Chapter 13: The Altar Holds (Sarah in zip ties; Blake burns the postcard and opens his hands).
